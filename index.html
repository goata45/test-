Our Story

Where craftsmanship still means something

Tucked into a Toronto workshop on Weston Rd, D & N Custom Upholstery & Furniture Refinishing has built a reputation the old-fashioned way — one carefully rebuilt frame, one hand-tied seam, one satisfied client at a time.

We're a full-service atelier: reupholstery and wood refinishing sit alongside custom furniture building, cabinetry, flooring and drapery, so a room can be reimagined from the frame up without juggling five different tradespeople. Every project starts with a real conversation about how you live, followed by work done entirely in-house by hand.

"A well-built frame can outlive three sofas. Our job is to make sure it does."

What We Do

Six crafts, one workshop

From a single reupholstered armchair to a full room refit, every discipline is handled by the same hands, start to finish.

01

Custom Reupholstery

Sofas, sectionals, dining chairs and heirloom pieces reupholstered in the fabric or leather of your choice.

02

Furniture Refinishing & Restoration

Wood frames stripped, repaired and refinished to restore structural integrity and original beauty.

03

Custom-Made Furniture

Bespoke sofas, chairs and headboards built to your exact specifications, from frame to fabric.

04

Cabinetry

Custom cabinetry crafted and finished to match your home's architecture and style.

05

Flooring

Flooring supply and installation, finished with the same attention to detail as our furniture work.

06

Drapery & Soft Furnishings

Custom drapery, cushions and soft furnishings tailored to complete the room.
